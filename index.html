What We Manufacture

Bags built for every use case

Every style below is fully customizable in size, colour and print — browse a category, then see it in our portfolio.

D-Cut Bags

Simple, sturdy and budget-friendly, with the handle die-cut directly into the bag body for a clean, seamless finish — ideal for retail, grocery and promotional use.

U-Cut Bags

A reinforced, curved handle cut-out spreads weight more evenly for heavier loads, with extra room across the face for custom branding.

Loop Handle Bags

Soft, stitched fabric handles give a comfortable grip and a more finished, retail-ready look — great for gifting, boutiques and events.

Laminated Bags

A glossy BOPP finish adds moisture and tear resistance while making colours and branding pop — a popular choice for premium retail and promotional bags.

Laundry Bags

Breathable, tear-resistant and built for heavy daily use — a reliable, reusable alternative to plastic for hotels, laundromats and households.

Cake Bags

Reinforced bases and handles keep cakes and pastries stable and secure, with a food-safe, presentable finish for bakery takeaway and gifting.

Non-Woven Caps

Lightweight and breathable, our caps are a hygienic, cost-effective choice for hospitals, food units and industrial safety kits.

Headrest Sheets

Durable yet easy to replace, our headrest sheets keep every seat looking fresh for airlines, railways and hospitality brands.

Thread & Shoe Bags

Compact, sturdy and simple to produce in bulk — a practical packaging solution for retail, gifting and everyday storage.

About Us

A decade of manufacturing eco-friendly bags

Who We Are

Innovative Overseas has been manufacturing eco-friendly non-woven bags for over a decade, built on a simple idea: give brands a sturdy, reusable alternative to plastic that still looks great. Based out of Delhi, our in-house production and printing setup lets us turn around custom sizes, colours and branding quickly, without compromising on quality.

What We Do

We manufacture non-woven products across nine core categories — from everyday D-Cut and U-Cut bags to laminated, laundry and cake bags, plus caps, headrest sheets and more.

  • D-Cut Bags
  • U-Cut Bags
  • Loop Handle Bags
  • Thread / Shoe Bags
  • Laundry Bags
  • Laminated Bags
  • Bath Fitting Bags
  • Non-Woven Caps
  • Cake Bags
  • Cushion Covers
  • Headrest Sheets
  • Non-Woven Rolls
